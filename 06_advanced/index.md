# Chapter 6: Advanced Features

This chapter explores advanced capabilities for power users, campaign module creators, and Game Masters, including Adventure Compendium packaging, granular permission matrices, and troubleshooting solutions.

---

## Chapter Overview

1. [**Adventure Compendium Export & Import**](./adventure_export.html)
   - Packaging entire guilds and contract networks into Foundry VTT Adventure Compendiums.
   - Automatic dependency scanning (attached scenes, monster actors, magic items, and journals).
   - Custom Adventure sheet badges and importing into fresh game worlds.

2. [**Visibility Matrix & Permissions**](./visibility_and_permissions.html)
   - The permission hierarchy: Game Master administration vs Player interaction.
   - Granular document ownership (Observer vs None) and player-specific access checkboxes.
   - Secure real-time socket communication and event validation.

3. [**FAQ & Troubleshooting**](./faq_and_troubleshooting.html)
   - Common questions regarding drag-and-drop, actor deletion cleanup, and theme syncing.
   - Resolving UI display anomalies and clearing application cache.
   - Verifying module compatibility with Foundry VTT V14 and D&D 5.5e.

---

> If you write and distribute third-party D&D 5e adventure modules, **DnD Guildhall's** Adventure Compendium integration allows you to package complete guildhalls with pre-written quest boards directly into your adventure compendium!
