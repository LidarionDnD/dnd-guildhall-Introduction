# The Trust & Reputation System

The **Trust & Reputation System** in **DnD Guildhall** gives Game Masters a tangible, reactive tool to represent the party's standing and political relationship with any guild on a continuous numerical scale from **-100 to +100**.

---

## 1. The Reputation Scale & Status Tiers

As the party completes contracts, fails missions, negotiates treaties, or commits transgressions, their standing shifts dynamically through five distinct status tiers:

| Status Tier | Value Range | Status Color | Narrative Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hostile** | `-100` to `-61` | `#e53935` (Red) | Outright enmity; guild members are adversarial or openly hostile. |
| **Suspicious** | `-60` to `-21` | `#ff7043` (Orange) | Distrust and caution; access to sensitive contracts and vaults is restricted. |
| **Neutral** | `-20` to `+20` | `#ffca28` (Yellow) | Standard commercial relationship; standard contracts and market rates. |
| **Friendly** | `+21` to `+60` | `#9ccc65` (Light Green) | Favored standing; access to lucrative missions, information, and safehouses. |
| **Allied** | `+61` to `+100` | `#66bb6a` (Green) | Blood oaths and highest esteem; full access to top-tier contracts and faction support. |

---

## 2. Visual Header Presentation

In the active guild headquarters, the party's standing is highlighted prominently in the header area:

- **The Trust Bar:** A horizontal reputation gauge spanning from extreme negative (Hostile) to extreme positive (Allied).
- **The Pin Marker:** An animated indicator positioned along the bar reflecting the exact mathematical percentage of the current trust score.
- **Trust Status Badge:** A color-coded status badge displaying the current status title (*Hostile*, *Suspicious*, *Neutral*, *Friendly*, or *Allied*) glowing in that tier's signature color.

---

## 3. Adjusting Trust in Settings

Game Masters can adjust a guild's trust score at any time:

1. Open the Guildhall and click the **"Settings"** tab (gear icon).
2. Navigate to the **"Trust"** section.
3. **Fine Adjustment:** Drag the **Trust Slider** or enter an exact integer value into the numeric input field.
4. **Quick Adjust Buttons:** Click the **+** or **-** buttons to quickly adjust the score after a quest outcome or roleplay scene.
5. Changes update live in the header for all connected players.

---

> **Tip for Game Masters:**
> Tie trust changes directly to quest outcomes! Successfully delivering a sensitive payload might award +15 Trust, while failing a stealth contract could deduct -20 Trust and push the party into Suspicious standing.
