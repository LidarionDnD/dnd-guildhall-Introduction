# Questboard Backgrounds & Board Styling

In addition to the 21 color themes, **DnD Guildhall** lets Game Masters customize the visual texture and surface of the 3-column quest board.

---

## 1. Configuring the Board Background

The quest board background setting is located at the very top of the **Design** settings panel:

1. Open the Guildhall and click the **"Settings"** tab (gear icon, visible to the Game Master).
2. Switch to the **"Design"** sub-tab.
3. Locate the **"Questboard Background"** dropdown at the top of the panel.
4. Select your preferred background option from the list.
5. Click **"Save Settings"** to apply the change live across the table.

---

## 2. The Three Background Modes

Game Masters can choose between three distinct styling modes:

### A. Corkboard (`cork`)
- The default, classic adventurers' bulletin board.
- Renders a warm, high-resolution corkboard texture (`Kork.webp`) with subtle vignette shading and realistic depth.

### B. Standard Theme (`default`)
- Integrates directly with the active color theme palette.
- Replaces the cork texture with a stylized, clean board surface that harmonizes with your chosen theme (e.g. dark obsidian for *Shadowfell*, cosmic blue for *Astral Plane*, or deep terminal alloy for *Cyberpunk*).

### C. Custom Image (`custom`)
- Allows complete freedom to upload or select any custom texture file (`.webp`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.svg`) using Foundry's FilePicker.
- Ideal for custom faction aesthetics such as carved dwarven stone, elven weirwood, polished royal marble, or ancient parchment.

---

## 3. Real-Time Board Synchronization

When the Game Master saves changes to the board background:

- The new background texture renders immediately on the **"Quests"** tab for all connected players.
- Changes persist across campaign sessions within the guild's document flags.
