# The 21 Pen & Paper Color Themes

**DnD Guildhall** includes **21 handcrafted Pen & Paper color themes** inspired by classic fantasy, dark gothic horror, cosmic mystery, cyberpunk sci-fi, and planar travel.

---

## 1. Choosing Themes in Settings

To configure a guild's color palette:

1. Open the Guildhall and click the **"Settings"** tab (gear icon, visible to the Game Master).
2. Switch to the **"Design"** sub-tab.
3. Scroll through the **Color Themes** list. Each theme occupies its own dedicated row with an icon, title, description, and live color preview swatches (Background, Panel, Accent, Text, and Parchment).
4. Click on any theme row to select it.
5. Click **"Save Settings"** at the bottom of the form. The theme is applied instantly!

## 3. Global Styling Architecture

When a theme is active, its color definitions propagate seamlessly through CSS variables across all module components:

- **Guildhall Application:** Headers, tab buttons, group borders, rank cards, and form inputs.
- **Guilds Overview Dashboard:** Overview background accents and guild card highlights.
- **Dedicated Quest Sidebar:** Frosted glass headers, borders, and count badges.
- **Parchment Quest Viewer:** Background vellum texture, scroll headers, text ink colors, and seal accents.
