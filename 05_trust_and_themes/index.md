# Chapter 5: Trust & Themes

Aesthetics and narrative reputation define the character of a guildhall. This chapter explores **DnD Guildhall's** comprehensive **Trust & Reputation System**, the visual customization options for the quest board, and the **21 Pen & Paper Color Themes**.

---

## Chapter Overview

1. [**The Trust & Reputation System**](./trust_system.html)
   - The continuous reputation scale from -100 to +100.
   - The five dynamic trust statuses: *Hostile*, *Suspicious*, *Neutral*, *Friendly*, and *Allied*.
   - The header Trust Bar, Pin Marker, and dynamic status badges.
   - Game Master controls (slider, number input, and quick adjust buttons).

2. [**The 21 Pen & Paper Color Themes**](./21_color_themes.html)
   - Overview of all 21 atmospheric theme presets.
   - The row-by-row preview interface with live color swatches.
   - How themes dynamically style the Guildhall app, overview dashboard, quest sidebar, and parchment viewer.

3. [**Questboard Backgrounds & Board Styling**](./questboard_backgrounds.html)
   - Configuring the quest board background in the Design settings.
   - The three background modes: *Corkboard*, *Standard Theme*, and *Custom Image*.
   - Setting custom textures via Foundry's FilePicker.

---

> **Tip for Game Masters:**
> Themes are configured on a per-guild basis. If your world features multiple factions—such as a holy paladin chapter and a gritty underworld thieves' syndicate—each guildhall can display completely different color palettes and background textures!
