# Chapter 4: Rules & Custom Tabs

Every legendary guild relies on a foundation of rules, codes of conduct, and clear progression criteria. This chapter details how Game Masters can document rank requirements, build custom regulation sections with custom icons, and expand the Guildhall with custom navigation tabs.

---

## Chapter Overview

1. [**Guild Ranks & Advancement Deeds**](./guild_ranks_rules.html)
   - The rank overview in responsive 480px cards.
   - Documenting promotion criteria, privileges, and duties in rich text.
   - Managing custom rank colors and hierarchy order.

2. [**Custom Rule Sections & Icons**](./custom_rule_sections.html)
   - Adding custom regulation categories (e.g. *Code of Conduct*, *Loot Distribution*, *Guild Charter*).
   - Assigning custom icons using FontAwesome or SVG/image file paths.
   - Editing section content with the full ProseMirror rich text editor.
   - Reordering and deleting rule sections.

3. [**Creating & Configuring Custom Tabs**](./custom_tabs.html)
   - Managing the tab navigation bar in Guild Settings.
   - Creating brand new custom tabs (e.g. *Hall of Fame*, *Crafting Circle*, *Allied Factions*).
   - Assigning custom tab icons (+30% enlarged in navigation).
   - Adding rich text content sections inside custom tabs.

---

> **Game Master Tip:**
> Custom tabs allow you to transform the Guildhall into a complete faction wiki. You can add dedicated lore pages, guild stronghold upgrades, crafting recipes, or ally directories directly inside the guild interface.
