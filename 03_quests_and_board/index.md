# Chapter 3: Quests & Quest Board

The contract board is the beating heart of any adventuring guild. This chapter provides a comprehensive guide to creating contracts, operating the interactive 3-column Kanban board, reviewing quests in the immersive parchment viewer, automating reward payouts, and utilizing the dedicated quest sidebar.

---

## Chapter Overview

1. [**The 3-Column Quest Board Workflow**](./questboard_workflow.html)
   - The three contract states: *Posted*, *Accepted*, and *Completed*.
   - Drag-and-drop mechanics with ghost preview.
   - GM context menu actions (sharing, visibility, duplication, transfer, deletion).

2. [**Creating Quests & Quest Editor**](./creating_quests.html)
   - Opening the Quest Editor sheet.
   - The 2x2 configuration matrix: Issuer, State, Guild Affiliation, and Required Rank.
   - Configuring coin rewards (PP, GP, SP, CP) and item drops.
   - Public rich text brief, private GM notes, and GM entity attachments.

3. [**The Immersive Quest Viewer**](./quest_viewer.html)
   - Parchment visual design and active color theme integration.
   - Player actions: Accepting, completing, and abandoning contracts.
   - Game Master controls: State toggles, locking, and live screen broadcasting.
   - Accessing secret GM notes and entity links.

4. [**Rewards & Automatic Payout Split**](./rewards_and_payout.html)
   - Currency rewards and linked magic items.
   - The Payout Rewards dialog.
   - Automated equal coin division and direct character sheet deposits.

5. [**The Dedicated Quest Sidebar**](./quest_sidebar.html)
   - Dedicated sidebar tab in Foundry's right navigation.
   - Guild accordions, World Quests, and state subgroups.
   - Real-time search filter and Expand All / Collapse All toggle.
   - Quick-hover parchment tooltip previews.

---

> **Tip for Game Masters:**
> Quests can either belong to a specific guild or be designated as unassigned **World Quests**. World Quests are accessible to all players regardless of guild membership and appear in the global section of the Quest Sidebar.
