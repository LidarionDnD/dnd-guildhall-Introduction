# Assigning Ranks & Roles

Guild progression thrives on titles, honors, and advancement. **DnD Guildhall** features a complete rank management system with custom color codes and dynamic ties to the quest board.

---

## 1. The Guild Rank System

Each guild utilizes a configured rank hierarchy where each tier is defined by name and HEX color. The default hierarchy includes:

| Rank | Default Color | Example Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Copper** | `#b87333` (Copper) | Novices, initiates, and probationers |
| **Silver** | `#c0c0c0` (Silver) | Proven adventurers with solid field experience |
| **Gold** | `#ffd700` (Gold) | Elite veterans for high-stakes missions |
| **Platinum** | `#e5e4e2` (Platinum) | Renowned champions and guild heroes |
| **Diamond** | `#b9f2ff` (Diamond) | Legendary figures, commanders, and masters |

---

## 2. Assigning a Rank to a Member

Assigning or promoting a member requires only two clicks:

1. Open the Guildhall and switch to the **"👥 Members"** tab.
2. Locate the member card of the desired character.
3. Click the **Rank Dropdown** on the member card.
4. Select the desired rank from the list.
5. The card updates instantly: the rank badge reflects the new title and the card border adopts the rank's custom color!

---

## 3. Integration with the Quest Board

The rank hierarchy links directly into contract eligibility:

- **Required Quest Rank:** When creating a quest, the Game Master can define a minimum rank requirement (e.g. *"Silver Rank or higher"*).
- **Advancement Motivation:** Players can consult the **"Rules"** tab at any time to see the criteria and deeds required for their next promotion.
- **Quest Viewer Badge:** In the Quest Viewer, the required rank badge glows in its distinctive rank color.

---

> **Tip for Game Masters:**
> Celebrate rank promotions in your campaign! When the party accomplishes a significant milestone, award their next guild rank in the Guildhall. The visual transformation of their member cards creates a tangible, rewarding sense of progression at the table.
