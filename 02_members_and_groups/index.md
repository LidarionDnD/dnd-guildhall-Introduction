# Chapter 2: Members & Groups

This chapter covers member enrollment, group management, and hierarchy configuration. You will learn how to assign characters to your guild using drag-and-drop, organize them into functional squads, and grant color-coded guild ranks.

---

## Chapter Overview

1. [**Assigning Characters & Drag-and-Drop**](./adding_characters.html)
   - Dragging actor documents directly from Foundry's sidebar into the guild.
   - Supported actor types (Player Characters, NPCs, Companions).
   - Removing members and automated database cleanup.

2. [**Creating & Organizing Groups**](./organizing_groups.html)
   - Structuring guilds into divisions (e.g. *Leadership*, *Strike Team*, *Initiates*).
   - Adding, renaming, reordering, and deleting groups.
   - Dragging members between groups.

3. [**Assigning Ranks & Roles**](./ranks_and_roles.html)
   - Assigning configured guild ranks to characters.
   - Visual styling with dynamic rank colors on member tiles.
   - How ranks interface with quest minimum requirements.

---

> **Tip for Game Masters:**
> You can enroll both player characters and allied NPCs in the same guildhall. This creates a cohesive, living community where players see their favorite allies alongside them!
