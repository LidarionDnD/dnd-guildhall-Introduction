# Creating & Organizing Groups

The Member Groups system in **DnD Guildhall** lets you structure your roster into functional divisions, tactical squads, or rank tiers.

---

## 1. Group Use Cases

Groups provide versatile ways to organize your roster:
- **Hierarchical Guilds:** Divide into *Leadership*, *Veterans*, *Full Members*, and *Initiates*.
- **West Marches Campaigns:** Organize players into multiple adventuring parties (e.g. *Squad A – Dragon Hunters*, *Squad B – Caravan Escort*).
- **Specialized Departments:** Create distinct teams such as *Arcane Researchers*, *Reconnaissance*, *Negotiators*, or *Crafting Circle*.

---

## 2. Creating a New Group

1. Open the Guildhall and switch to the **"👥 Members"** tab.
2. In the top right corner of the member roster, click **"+ Add Group"**.
3. In the input dialog, type your desired group name (e.g. *"High Council"* or *"Vanguard Strike Force"*).
4. Click **"Save"**.
5. The new group block appears immediately with its own header and empty member drop zone.

---

## 3. Renaming Groups

1. Hover over the title of the group you wish to rename.
2. Click the small **pen icon** (`fas fa-pen` – *Edit Name*) next to the group name.
3. Enter the updated name in the dialog and click **"Save"**.

---

## 4. Reordering Groups (Sorting)

You can reorder group sections vertically to highlight your most important divisions:

- <i class="fas fa-arrow-up"></i> **Move Up:** Moves the group up by one position.
- <i class="fas fa-arrow-down"></i> **Move Down:** Moves the group down by one position.

---

## 5. Moving Members Between Groups

To transfer a member to a different group:
1. Click and hold the member card you want to move.
2. Drag the card into the target group container.
3. Release the mouse button. The member card transfers immediately.

---

## 6. Deleting a Group

1. Click the red **trash can icon** (`fas fa-trash`) in the group header.
2. Confirm the safety dialog prompt.
3. Upon confirmation, the group container is removed.

> **Tip:**
> If a group still contains members when deleted, you can redistribute them to other groups prior to deletion or reassign them afterwards.
